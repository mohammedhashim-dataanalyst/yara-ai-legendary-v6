# Honest limits

- Photoreal VRM face requires a Blender-created asset. The included procedural avatar is real, animated, and stylised.
- AI-generated photo scenes are not free on-device. Ambient scenes are procedural vector art rendered and animated by Canvas.
- Online multiplayer and downloadable game packs require a server. The included games are on-device games versus Yara.
- ML Kit emotion support is best-effort smile probability, not full emotion classification.
